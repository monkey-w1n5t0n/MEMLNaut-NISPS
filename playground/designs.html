NISPS Playground

Neural Interactive Shaping of Parameter Spaces

  • Map a 2D joystick to 20 visual parameters or 126 synthesizer parameters through a neural network
  • Two learning modes: show examples of what you want, or give thumbs up/down feedback
  • The network learns to interpolate — move the joystick and the entire parameter space responds
  • You don't need to understand the parameters — just explore and teach
